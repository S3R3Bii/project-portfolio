Banking Intent Atlas compares two working classifiers across 77 banking intents. It connects the technical question—how accurately can we classify a query?—to an operational one: how much should we refer for human review?

01 / The decision

Fewer manual reviews.
Visible mistakes.

The frozen sentence encoder improved accuracy by 4.97 percentage points over a TF-IDF baseline on the same held-out queries. Its separately selected policy accepted 2,755 and referred 325; 96 accepted suggestions were wrong.

Recommendation: the encoder is the stronger candidate for a supervised pilot under this study’s illustrative error target. Keep a human escalation route and add a validated domain check before operational use.

This is an offline benchmark, not a deployed banking service. Coverage is not measured labor savings. The encoder confidently accepted 2 of 12 synthetic non-banking probes.

04 / Engineering choices

Separate the data.
Lock the policy.
Then measure.

Python · SQL · scikit-learn · ONNX Runtime · pretrained embeddings · probability calibration

  1. Audit the sourceRemove 62 overlapping, conflicting or duplicate training rows. Preserve the official test; report a separate sensitivity check.
  2. Fit two pipelines6,958 queries train a word TF-IDF baseline and a frozen 384-dimensional MiniLM representation, each with logistic regression.
  3. Calibrate, then choose1,491 queries fit temperature scaling. Another 1,492 select the model and confidence cutoff. Both are locked before final testing.
  4. Reconcile the evidence35 independent checks reconstruct metrics and intervals, confirm split isolation, and reconcile 6,160 model/query records through SQL.

No customer identifiers or timestamps are available, so this is not a customer-group or temporal holdout. Exact-normalized deduplication does not remove every paraphrase.

05 / What comes next

Prove it on new queries.

A prospective pilot should use a permitted sample from a new institution or time period, include unknown intents, and measure review time rather than infer it from benchmark coverage. Select error tolerances with the team that bears their cost.

The current project demonstrates an executed NLP workflow and inspectable evidence. It makes no claim of research novelty, production adoption, financial impact or a universal safety guarantee.
