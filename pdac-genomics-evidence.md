# PDAC Genomics Evidence

Make evidence attrition, coverage and comparator sensitivity explicit before advancing a biological claim.

**Evidence snapshot:** Working manuscript; not peer reviewed

![PDAC Genomics Evidence — demonstrated interface or analytical output](images/pdac-genomics-evidence.jpg)

Earlier v7 evidence-browser capture. The v8 reader retains this dashboard and adds a direct comparator audit; independent biological validation remains unresolved.

## What the work demonstrates

An AI-assisted audit quantifies evidence attrition, measured-data coverage and comparator sensitivity. Prior research already integrated these evidence layers and used the same three expression/compartment datasets.

## Scope and next step

A descriptive, observational audit with limited biological novelty. No independent biological validation, pancreatic-specific target, equivalence, treatment benefit or journal-readiness claim. Full reader archive is not hosted; individual reports and tables are available.
