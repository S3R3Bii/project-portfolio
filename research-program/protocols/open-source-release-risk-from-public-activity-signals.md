# Open-source release risk from public activity signals

## Execution update — 30 October 2026

A scoped study is executed and released at https://github.com/S3R3Bii/release-cadence-review. On 39 final release origins from four other projects, the frozen cadence rule lowers macro Brier from 0.2861623152 to 0.2497268094 and micro Brier from 0.2230423210 to 0.1917280829. Its predeclared 0.5 review threshold emits no flags and misses all ten observed 90-day gaps. Brier improves for each final project while encode/httpx log loss worsens. The release preserves the failed review policy and recommends contextual review rather than automatic routing. Read its fixed executable protocol, complete exclusions and validation. The broader proposal below is retained as historical design context; it is not the release specification. Independent program manuscripts are public after checks; separate formal-publication projects remain private.

## Decision

Do release-cadence and contributor-concentration signals predict maintenance interruptions on unseen projects?

## Target data

- [GitHub public event archive](https://www.gharchive.org/) — targeted; exact export and rights must be verified.

## Study design

Repository-level survival models.

Repository and future-time holdouts; bots and forks handled; avoid developer-level employment judgments.

## Contribution to test

Distinguish project resilience from raw popularity. This is a proposed contribution, not a verified gap in the literature. Prior work must be reviewed before any novelty claim.

## Evidence to retain

- Exact source snapshot, request, retrieval time, observation window, definitions, units and redistribution terms.
- Prespecified splits, baseline, search space, tuning budget, seed and decision metric.
- All candidate results, including failures; final holdout used only after model selection.
- Independent headline recomputation, leakage checks and reviewed figures with units and uncertainty.
- Reproduction command and environment; private LaTeX manuscript written from measured results.
