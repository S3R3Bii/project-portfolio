# Freight rerouting under concentration stress

## Execution update — 29 October 2026

A scoped study is executed and released at https://github.com/S3R3Bii/freight-substitution-stress. Across all 16 held primary commodity/closure cases, conditional recovery ranges from 0% to 100% under fixed hypothetical headroom and strict support. Exact optimization improves on the specified greedy baseline in 27 of 256 held settings, including 3 of 16 primary cases; no operational capacity or realized benefit is established. Read its fixed executable protocol, complete exclusions and validation. The broader proposal below is retained as historical design context; it is not the release specification. Independent program manuscripts are public after checks; separate formal-publication projects remain private.
