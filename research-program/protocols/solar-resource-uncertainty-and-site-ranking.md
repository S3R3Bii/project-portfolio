# Solar resource uncertainty and site ranking

## Execution update — 30 October 2026

A scoped study is executed and released at https://github.com/S3R3Bii/solar-resource-shortlist. The frozen ten-year mean has zero held western shortlist regret and 0.005710 kWh/m²/day eastern regret. All six candidates match the best western pair in every held year, and four rules tie on the eastern shortlist score. Thirty-day common-calendar removals change 4 of 84 western and 6 of 84 eastern shortlists; a one-day gap changes one close eastern comparison. These artificial cases establish no unique rule advantage, outage probability, plant output or financial return. Read its fixed executable protocol, complete exclusions and validation. The broader proposal below is retained as historical design context; it is not the release specification. Independent program manuscripts are public after checks; separate formal-publication projects remain private.

## Decision

How sensitive is a solar site shortlist to interannual variability and missing weather observations?

## Target data

- [National Solar Radiation Database](https://nsrdb.nrel.gov/) — targeted; exact export and rights must be verified.

## Study design

Spatial uncertainty and robust ranking.

Multi-year blocks; held-out stations where available; explicit radiation-to-output assumptions.

## Contribution to test

Distinguish resource screening from financial feasibility and actual plant performance. This is a proposed contribution, not a verified gap in the literature. Prior work must be reviewed before any novelty claim.

## Evidence to retain

- Exact source snapshot, request, retrieval time, observation window, definitions, units and redistribution terms.
- Prespecified splits, baseline, search space, tuning budget, seed and decision metric.
- All candidate results, including failures; final holdout used only after model selection.
- Independent headline recomputation, leakage checks and reviewed figures with units and uncertainty.
- Reproduction command and environment; private LaTeX manuscript written from measured results.

geospatial statistics, uncertainty, scenario analysis. The repository must demonstrate these through executed work before they become portfolio claims.
