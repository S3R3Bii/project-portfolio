# Industrial inspection queues under selective observation

## Execution update — 30 October 2026

A scoped study is executed and released at https://github.com/S3R3Bii/inspection-label-coverage. In a captured current cohort of 183,500 operating air sources, 13,613 (7.4%) have a recorded PSS/FAI stack-test result during 2021-2025; 169,698 have no dated stack-test record. On-site evaluations cover 41,059 sources, with 4,562 off-site-only and 137,879 without a dated evaluation. Full state/class summaries support source reconciliation before model development. Missing records do not supply passing labels; required testing and operational value remain unverified. Read its fixed executable protocol, complete exclusions and validation. The broader proposal below is retained as historical design context; it is not the release specification. Independent program manuscripts are public after checks; separate formal-publication projects remain private.

## Decision

How much can an inspection model be trusted when outcomes exist mainly for inspected facilities?

## Target data

- [EPA Enforcement and Compliance History Online](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads) — targeted; exact export and rights must be verified.

## Study design

Selection-bias diagnostics and bounded risk ranking.

Time and facility holdouts; coverage maps; inverse-weighting sensitivity only with defensible propensity inputs.

## Contribution to test

Identify where an automated queue must abstain because labels are selectively observed. This is a proposed contribution, not a verified gap in the literature. Prior work must be reviewed before any novelty claim.

## Evidence to retain

- Exact source snapshot, request, retrieval time, observation window, definitions, units and redistribution terms.
- Prespecified splits, baseline, search space, tuning budget, seed and decision metric.
- All candidate results, including failures; final holdout used only after model selection.
- Independent headline recomputation, leakage checks and reviewed figures with units and uncertainty.
- Reproduction command and environment; private LaTeX manuscript written from measured results.

## Skills

ML evaluation, selection bias, calibration, SQL. The repository must demonstrate these through executed work before they become portfolio claims.
