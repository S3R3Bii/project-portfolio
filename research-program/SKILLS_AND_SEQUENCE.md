# Evidence employers can inspect

The program prioritizes work that demonstrates a decision process, not just familiarity with a library. Project statements stay independent-research statements; they do not stand in for years of professional production experience.

| Hiring need | Evidence to make visible | First projects |

## Current source grounding

Google's [Business and Marketing Data Scientist posting](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/133000976167510726-business-and-marketing-data-scientist) specifically calls for Python/R data manipulation, SQL, statistical work, extracting and validating data, and explaining statistical concepts to nontechnical audiences. It motivates the first three rows above; the study does not claim that a new portfolio project fulfills a professional tenure requirement.

Trexquant's [Senior Data Engineer posting](https://apply.workable.com/trexquant/j/77815068E5/) emphasizes Python/SQL pipelines, normalization, quality control, monitoring, financial data and columnar storage. Its [Python Engineer—Trade Operations posting](https://apply.workable.com/trexquant/j/F508325449/) emphasizes Python, Linux, incident analysis and trading infrastructure. These help select demonstrable engineering skills. Both also require specific professional experience that this portfolio must not imply James already has.

Verified from official job pages on 28 October 2026. This is a skills map, not a claim that every named vacancy is qualified or still open at a later date.

## First five execution priorities

1. Complaint capacity: SQL, temporal validation and decision value in a small fully inspectable study.
2. Point-in-time financial statements: a demanding data-engineering problem with measurable leakage tests.
3. Electricity forecast correction: operational forecasting with release-time and calibration controls.
4. Kinematic event detection: a direct connection to movement analysis and subject/site generalization.
5. Fixed-budget model selection: tuning quality, reproducibility and compute efficiency across datasets.

The remaining projects broaden the evidence rather than repeating these five under new titles. Dataset terms and feasibility determine execution order. Reviewed independent manuscripts may accompany their GitHub study packages; formal-publication projects remain private.
