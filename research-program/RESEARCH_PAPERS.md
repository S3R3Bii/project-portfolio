These independent, AI-assisted research manuscripts explain the decision, measured finding, methods and limitations behind the released studies. They have not been peer reviewed or accepted for formal publication. Each link is pinned to the manuscript revision that passed its current research replay.

Manuscripts intended for formal publication, including the separate Azure optimization study, remain private. Third-party source papers, excluded datasets and private records are not published here.
