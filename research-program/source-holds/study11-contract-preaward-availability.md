# Contract review needs inputs available before award

A pre-award warning could help a contracting team plan its review workload. It needs evidence that each input existed before the award decision.

**Current state: timing evidence is missing; no classifier or contract ranking has been produced.**

The official [transaction API contract](https://github.com/fedspendingtransparency/usaspending-api/blob/master/usaspending_api/api_contracts/contracts/v2/transactions.md) exposes action dates, action types and modification numbers. An action date describes a transaction; it does not establish when a field became available to an analyst.

The [USAspending source disclosure](https://www.usaspending.gov/data/about-the-data-download.pdf), updated 6 October 2026, describes contract publication after award/modification, generally within five days, with longer exceptions including DOD/USACE delays. A current award summary may also contain later changes. The inspected evidence therefore does not establish the pre-award inputs required by this study.

Before collecting a modeling cohort, define the prediction date, prove feature availability and revisions, establish stable award/base-action identities, and fix the follow-up window for modification labels. Any output remains a workload review aid; vendor eligibility and fraud judgments are outside scope. The broader historical proposal is preserved. No result, savings or completed-study claim is made.
