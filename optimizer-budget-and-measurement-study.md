# Optimizer Budget and Measurement Study

Four controlled experiments compare how to spend a limited optimization budget, including 100, 500 and 1,000 variables.

**Evidence snapshot:** Author-review candidate; not submitted or peer reviewed

## What the work demonstrates

The adaptive experiment compares an online trigger with a fixed within-block permutation of assigned cutoffs and a matched Powell restart. This measures the performance of the configured decision rules while separating several alternative explanations; native early termination remains part of their behavior.

## Scope and next step

Descriptive evidence for fixed implementations and finite synthetic benchmark panels. Donor cycles and repeated budgets create dependencies. No general hybrid superiority, population-significance, practical-equivalence, field-firstness, production saving or cloud-speed claim follows. Wider scheduling research already exists; the exact empirical contribution and journal acceptance remain editorial judgments.
