# Open Research Commons

A shared research record for people and agents: define a question, attach evidence, run a supported analysis, inspect independent checks and publish a frozen result.

**Evidence snapshot:** Hosted numerical-research workflow

## What the work demonstrates

MCP tools; authenticated writes; immutable releases; provenance; numerical recomputation.

## Scope and next step

The demonstrated workflow is a bounded synthetic regression study, not a general autonomous scientist.
